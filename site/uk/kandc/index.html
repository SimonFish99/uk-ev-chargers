EV charging in K&C — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in K&C?

There are 19 public EV charging points in K&C: 0 rapid (50kW+), 19 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in K&C?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in K&C right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in K&C?

Charge points in K&C are run by networks including Total Energies (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in K&C free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
