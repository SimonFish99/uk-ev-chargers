EV Chargers in Southall

22 stations 2 rapid (50kW+) 16 fast (7–50kW) 4 slow

Southall has 22 public EV charging points, including 2 rapid (50kW+) and 16 fast (7–50kW) chargers. The highest-powered charge point listed here is McDonald's Hanwell at 120kW. Charge points are operated by BP Pulse (UK), E.ON Drive and Shell Recharge Solutions (UK), plus 3 other networks. The most widely available connector in Southall is Type 2 (Socket Only).

EV charging in Southall — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Southall?

There are 22 public EV charging points in Southall: 2 rapid (50kW+), 16 fast (7–50kW) and 4 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 3 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Southall?

Southall has 2 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is McDonald's Hanwell at 120kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Southall?

Charge points in Southall are run by 6 networks, the largest being BP Pulse (UK), E.ON Drive and Shell Recharge Solutions (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Southall?

Of the 22 charge points listed in Southall, 14 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 8 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Southall free to use?

Most of the 22 charge points in Southall require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
