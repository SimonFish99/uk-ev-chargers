EV charging in York — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in York?

There are 25 public EV charging points in York: 7 rapid (50kW+), 18 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in York?

York has 7 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 60kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in York?

Charge points in York are run by networks including BP Pulse (UK), Charge Your Car and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in York free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
