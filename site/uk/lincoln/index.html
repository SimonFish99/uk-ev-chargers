EV charging in Lincoln — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Lincoln?

There are 25 public EV charging points in Lincoln: 8 rapid (50kW+), 17 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 3 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Lincoln?

Lincoln has 8 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Tesla Lincoln at 250kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Lincoln?

Charge points in Lincoln are run by 12 networks, the largest being POD Point (UK), Fuuse and BP Pulse (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Lincoln?

Of the 25 charge points listed in Lincoln, 3 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 9 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Lincoln free to use?

Most of the 25 charge points in Lincoln require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
