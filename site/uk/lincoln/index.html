EV charging in Lincoln — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Lincoln?

There are 26 public EV charging points in Lincoln: 6 rapid (50kW+), 19 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Lincoln?

Lincoln has 6 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 250kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Lincoln?

Charge points in Lincoln are run by networks including BP Pulse (UK), POD Point (UK) and Connected Kerb. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Lincoln free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
