EV charging in Newcastle upon Tyne — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Newcastle upon Tyne?

There are 62 public EV charging points in Newcastle upon Tyne: 9 rapid (50kW+), 47 fast (7–50kW) and 6 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Newcastle upon Tyne?

Newcastle upon Tyne has 9 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 175kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Newcastle upon Tyne?

Charge points in Newcastle upon Tyne are run by networks including Charge Your Car, BP Pulse (UK) and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Newcastle upon Tyne free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
