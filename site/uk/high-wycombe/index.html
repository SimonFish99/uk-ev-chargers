EV charging in High Wycombe — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in High Wycombe?

There are 8 public EV charging points in High Wycombe: 4 rapid (50kW+), 3 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in High Wycombe?

High Wycombe has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 120kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in High Wycombe?

Charge points in High Wycombe are run by networks including MER, Osprey Charging and Roam Charging (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in High Wycombe free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
