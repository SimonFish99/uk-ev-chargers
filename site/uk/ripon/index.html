EV charging in Ripon — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Ripon?

There are 9 public EV charging points in Ripon: 2 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Ripon?

Ripon has 2 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Ripon?

Charge points in Ripon are run by networks including The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ), Project EV and Roam Charging (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Ripon free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
