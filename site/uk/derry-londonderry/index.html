EV charging in Derry / Londonderry — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Derry / Londonderry?

There are 5 public EV charging points in Derry / Londonderry: 0 rapid (50kW+), 5 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 1 different connector type.

Are there rapid chargers in Derry / Londonderry?

No — there are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Derry / Londonderry right now. The fastest available is 22kW, at Strand Road, which would add roughly 66 miles of range in an hour. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Derry / Londonderry?

Charge points in Derry / Londonderry are run by 3 networks, the largest being ESB Ecars, Weev and ChargePoint. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Derry / Londonderry?

Of the 5 charge points listed in Derry / Londonderry, 4 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Derry / Londonderry free to use?

Most of the 5 charge points in Derry / Londonderry require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
