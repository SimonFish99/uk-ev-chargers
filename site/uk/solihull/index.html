EV charging in Solihull — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Solihull?

There are 26 public EV charging points in Solihull: 4 rapid (50kW+), 21 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Solihull?

Solihull has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 150kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Solihull?

Charge points in Solihull are run by networks including Evolt Network (Swarco E.Connect), BP Pulse (UK) and The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Solihull free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
