EV Chargers in Worthing

14 stations 4 rapid (50kW+) 9 fast (7–50kW) 1 slow

Worthing has 14 public EV charging points, including 4 rapid (50kW+) and 9 fast (7–50kW) chargers. The highest-powered charge point listed here is BP Petrol Forecourt at 150kW. Charge points are operated by Connected Kerb, BP Pulse (UK) and ChargePoint, plus 2 other networks. The most widely available connector in Worthing is Type 2 (Socket Only).

EV charging in Worthing — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Worthing?

There are 14 public EV charging points in Worthing: 4 rapid (50kW+), 9 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 4 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Worthing?

Worthing has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is BP Petrol Forecourt at 150kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Worthing?

Charge points in Worthing are run by 5 networks, the largest being Connected Kerb, BP Pulse (UK) and ChargePoint. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Worthing?

Of the 14 charge points listed in Worthing, 3 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 1 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Worthing free to use?

Most of the 14 charge points in Worthing require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
