EV charging in Bishop Auckland — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Bishop Auckland?

There are 8 public EV charging points in Bishop Auckland: 3 rapid (50kW+), 3 fast (7–50kW) and 2 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Bishop Auckland?

Bishop Auckland has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 150kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Bishop Auckland?

Charge points in Bishop Auckland are run by networks including MER, POD Point (UK) and Smart Charge by Sainsbury's (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Bishop Auckland free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
