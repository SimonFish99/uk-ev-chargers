EV charging in Barry — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Barry?

There are 18 public EV charging points in Barry: 1 rapid (50kW+), 17 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Barry?

Barry has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Barry?

Charge points in Barry are run by networks including Connected Kerb, The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ) and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Barry free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
