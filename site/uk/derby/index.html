EV charging in Derby — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Derby?

There are 33 public EV charging points in Derby: 14 rapid (50kW+), 18 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Derby?

Derby has 14 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 175kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Derby?

Charge points in Derby are run by networks including ChargePoint, BP Pulse (UK) and Chargemaster. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Derby free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
