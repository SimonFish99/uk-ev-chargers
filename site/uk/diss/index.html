EV charging in Diss — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Diss?

There are 6 public EV charging points in Diss: 1 rapid (50kW+), 5 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Diss?

Diss has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 60kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Diss?

Charge points in Diss are run by networks including ElectRoad (UK), ChargePoint and The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Diss free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
