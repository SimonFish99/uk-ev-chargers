10 stations 3 rapid (50kW+) 7 fast (7–50kW)

EV charging in St Albans — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in St Albans?

There are 10 public EV charging points in St Albans: 3 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 4 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in St Albans?

St Albans has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Griffiths Retail Park at 300kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in St Albans?

Charge points in St Albans are run by 7 networks, the largest being BP Pulse (UK), Evolt Network (Swarco E.Connect) and Monta. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in St Albans?

Of the 10 charge points listed in St Albans, 4 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 1 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in St Albans free to use?

Most of the 10 charge points in St Albans require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
