EV charging in Rotherham — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Rotherham?

There are 25 public EV charging points in Rotherham: 7 rapid (50kW+), 18 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Rotherham?

Rotherham has 7 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 180kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Rotherham?

Charge points in Rotherham are run by networks including Shell Recharge Solutions (UK), POD Point (UK) and InstaVolt Ltd. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Rotherham free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
