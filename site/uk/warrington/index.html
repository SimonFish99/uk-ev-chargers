EV charging in Warrington — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Warrington?

There are 19 public EV charging points in Warrington: 4 rapid (50kW+), 14 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Warrington?

Warrington has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 150kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Warrington?

Charge points in Warrington are run by networks including BP Pulse (UK), Connected Kerb and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Warrington free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
