EV charging in Colchester — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Colchester?

There are 35 public EV charging points in Colchester: 10 rapid (50kW+), 24 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Colchester?

Colchester has 10 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 300kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Colchester?

Charge points in Colchester are run by networks including POD Point (UK), Plug N Go Ltd and BP Pulse (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Colchester free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
