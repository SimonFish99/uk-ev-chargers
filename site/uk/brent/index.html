EV charging in Brent — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Brent?

There are 43 public EV charging points in Brent: 0 rapid (50kW+), 41 fast (7–50kW) and 2 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Brent?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Brent right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Brent?

Charge points in Brent are run by networks including Total Energies (UK) and Shell Recharge Solutions (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Brent free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
