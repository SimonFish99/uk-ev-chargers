EV charging in Worksop — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Worksop?

There are 6 public EV charging points in Worksop: 3 rapid (50kW+), 3 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Worksop?

Worksop has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 360kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Worksop?

Charge points in Worksop are run by networks including BP Pulse (UK), The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ) and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Worksop free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
