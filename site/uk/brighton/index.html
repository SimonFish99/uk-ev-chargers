EV charging in Brighton — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Brighton?

There are 264 public EV charging points in Brighton: 1 rapid (50kW+), 31 fast (7–50kW) and 232 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Brighton?

Brighton has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Brighton?

Charge points in Brighton are run by networks including Blink Charging (UK), POD Point (UK) and EB Charging. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Brighton free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
