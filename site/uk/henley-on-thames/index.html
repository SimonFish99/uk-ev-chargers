How many EV charging points are there in Henley-on-Thames?

Henley-on-Thames has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.
