EV charging in Chelsea — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Chelsea?

Are there rapid chargers in Chelsea?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Chelsea right now — the fastest available is 4.8kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Chelsea?

Charge points in Chelsea are run by networks including Shell Recharge Solutions (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Chelsea free to use?
