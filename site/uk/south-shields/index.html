EV charging in South Shields — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in South Shields?

There are 20 public EV charging points in South Shields: 4 rapid (50kW+), 13 fast (7–50kW) and 3 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in South Shields?

South Shields has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in South Shields?

Charge points in South Shields are run by networks including Charge Your Car, MER and Evolt Network (Swarco E.Connect). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in South Shields free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
