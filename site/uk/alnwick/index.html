EV charging in Alnwick — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Alnwick?

There are 8 public EV charging points in Alnwick: 3 rapid (50kW+), 5 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Alnwick?

Alnwick has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 350kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Alnwick?

Charge points in Alnwick are run by networks including Charge Your Car, EO Charging and Connected Kerb. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Alnwick free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
