EV charging in Hythe — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Hythe?

There are 9 public EV charging points in Hythe: 1 rapid (50kW+), 8 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Hythe?

Hythe has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 120kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Hythe?

Charge points in Hythe are run by networks including Joju Ltd, Connected Kerb and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Hythe free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
