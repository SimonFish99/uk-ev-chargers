EV charging in Wakefield — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Wakefield?

There are 15 public EV charging points in Wakefield: 13 rapid (50kW+), 2 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Wakefield?

Wakefield has 13 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 200kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Wakefield?

Charge points in Wakefield are run by networks including The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ), BP Pulse (UK) and Osprey Charging. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Wakefield free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
