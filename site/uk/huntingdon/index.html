EV Chargers in Huntingdon

10 stations 3 rapid (50kW+) 6 fast (7–50kW) 1 slow

Huntingdon has 10 public EV charging points, including 3 rapid (50kW+) and 6 fast (7–50kW) chargers. The highest-powered charge point listed here is Shell Buckden at 175kW. Charge points are operated by POD Point (UK), MFG EV Power and Smart Charge by Sainsbury's (UK), plus 1 other network. The most widely available connector in Huntingdon is Type 2 (Socket Only).

EV charging in Huntingdon — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Huntingdon?

There are 10 public EV charging points in Huntingdon: 3 rapid (50kW+), 6 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 3 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Huntingdon?

Huntingdon has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Shell Buckden at 175kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Huntingdon?

Charge points in Huntingdon are run by 4 networks, the largest being POD Point (UK), MFG EV Power and Smart Charge by Sainsbury's (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Huntingdon?

Of the 10 charge points listed in Huntingdon, 3 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 5 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Huntingdon free to use?

Most of the 10 charge points in Huntingdon require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
