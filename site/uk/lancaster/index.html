EV charging in Lancaster — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Lancaster?

There are 17 public EV charging points in Lancaster: 6 rapid (50kW+), 11 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Lancaster?

Lancaster has 6 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 350kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Lancaster?

Charge points in Lancaster are run by networks including EO Charging, Shell Recharge Solutions (UK) and Blink Charging (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Lancaster free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
