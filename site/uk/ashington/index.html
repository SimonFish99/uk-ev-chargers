EV charging in Ashington — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Ashington?

There are 11 public EV charging points in Ashington: 2 rapid (50kW+), 9 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Ashington?

Ashington has 2 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Ashington?

Charge points in Ashington are run by networks including EO Charging, Charge Your Car and evcharge.online. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Ashington free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
