EV charging in Wigan — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Wigan?

There are 12 public EV charging points in Wigan: 9 rapid (50kW+), 3 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Wigan?

Wigan has 9 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 300kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Wigan?

Charge points in Wigan are run by networks including POD Point (UK), Osprey Charging and ChargePoint. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Wigan free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
