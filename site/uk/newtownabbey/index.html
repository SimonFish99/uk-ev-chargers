EV charging in Newtownabbey — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Newtownabbey?

There are 11 public EV charging points in Newtownabbey: 4 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 3 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Newtownabbey?

Newtownabbey has 4 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is HRL Glenwell at 150kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Newtownabbey?

Charge points in Newtownabbey are run by 2 networks, the largest being Weev and BP Pulse (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Newtownabbey?

Of the 11 charge points listed in Newtownabbey, 8 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Newtownabbey free to use?

Most of the 11 charge points in Newtownabbey require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
