EV charging in Thornbury — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Thornbury?

There are 5 public EV charging points in Thornbury: 0 rapid (50kW+), 5 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Thornbury?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Thornbury right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Thornbury?

Charge points in Thornbury are run by networks including The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ), ChargePoint and Hubsta. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Thornbury free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
