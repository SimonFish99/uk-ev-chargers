EV charging in Harlow — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Harlow?

There are 7 public EV charging points in Harlow: 0 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Harlow?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Harlow right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Harlow?

Charge points in Harlow are run by networks including POD Point (UK), BP Pulse (UK) and ChargePoint. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Harlow free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
