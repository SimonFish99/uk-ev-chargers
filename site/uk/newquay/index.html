EV charging in Newquay — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Newquay?

There are 5 public EV charging points in Newquay: 1 rapid (50kW+), 4 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 4 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Newquay?

Newquay has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Shell Carland Cross at 50kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Newquay?

Charge points in Newquay are run by 4 networks, the largest being Go Zero (GB), The GeniePoint Network (EQUANS EV Solutions) and BP Pulse (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Newquay?

Of the 5 charge points listed in Newquay, 1 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 2 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Newquay free to use?

Most of the 5 charge points in Newquay require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
