EV charging in Thornton Heath — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Thornton Heath?

There are 10 public EV charging points in Thornton Heath: 0 rapid (50kW+), 9 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 1 different connector type.

Are there rapid chargers in Thornton Heath?

No — there are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Thornton Heath right now. The fastest available is 22kW, at Beulah Crescent, which would add roughly 66 miles of range in an hour. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Thornton Heath?

Charge points in Thornton Heath are run by 2 networks, the largest being Believ and Char.gy. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Thornton Heath?

Of the 10 charge points listed in Thornton Heath, 9 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app). Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Thornton Heath free to use?

Most of the 10 charge points in Thornton Heath require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
