EV charging in Bexley — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Bexley?

There are 8 public EV charging points in Bexley: 0 rapid (50kW+), 8 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 1 different connector type.

Are there rapid chargers in Bexley?

No — there are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Bexley right now. The fastest available is 22kW, at Hurst Grid Substation Site, which would add roughly 66 miles of range in an hour. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Bexley?

Charge points in Bexley are run by 2 networks, the largest being Blink Charging (UK) and POD Point (UK). The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Bexley?

Of the 8 charge points listed in Bexley, 7 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 1 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Bexley free to use?

Most of the 8 charge points in Bexley require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
