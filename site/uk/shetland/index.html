EV charging in Shetland — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Shetland?

There are 9 public EV charging points in Shetland: 0 rapid (50kW+), 9 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Shetland?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Shetland right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Shetland?

Charge points in Shetland are run by networks including Chargeplace Scotland, POD Point (UK) and Charge Your Car. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Shetland free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
