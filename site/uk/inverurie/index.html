EV charging in Inverurie — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Inverurie?

There are 6 public EV charging points in Inverurie: 1 rapid (50kW+), 5 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Inverurie?

Inverurie has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 100kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Inverurie?

Are the EV chargers in Inverurie free to use?
