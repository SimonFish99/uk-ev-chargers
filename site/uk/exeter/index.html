EV charging in Exeter — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Exeter?

There are 47 public EV charging points in Exeter: 10 rapid (50kW+), 37 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Exeter?

Exeter has 10 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 360kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Exeter?

Charge points in Exeter are run by networks including Wenea, POD Point (UK) and Shell Recharge Solutions (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Exeter free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
