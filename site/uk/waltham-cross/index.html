EV charging in Waltham Cross — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Waltham Cross?

There are 8 public EV charging points in Waltham Cross: 1 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 3 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Waltham Cross?

Waltham Cross has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Lidl-Waltham Cross at 50kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Waltham Cross?

Charge points in Waltham Cross are run by 3 networks, the largest being Blink Charging (UK), BP Pulse (UK) and Lidl. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Waltham Cross?

Of the 8 charge points listed in Waltham Cross, 4 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 3 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Waltham Cross free to use?

Most of the 8 charge points in Waltham Cross require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
