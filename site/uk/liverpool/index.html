EV charging in Liverpool — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Liverpool?

There are 335 public EV charging points in Liverpool: 11 rapid (50kW+), 21 fast (7–50kW) and 303 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Liverpool?

Liverpool has 11 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 350kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Liverpool?

Charge points in Liverpool are run by networks including Shell Recharge Solutions (UK), POD Point (UK) and ChargePoint. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Liverpool free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
