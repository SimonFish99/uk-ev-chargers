EV charging in Hungerford — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Hungerford?

There are 15 public EV charging points in Hungerford: 3 rapid (50kW+), 3 fast (7–50kW) and 9 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Hungerford?

Hungerford has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 160kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Hungerford?

Charge points in Hungerford are run by networks including Shell Recharge Solutions (UK), ubitricity and POD Point (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Hungerford free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
