EV charging in Penrith — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Penrith?

There are 13 public EV charging points in Penrith: 3 rapid (50kW+), 9 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Penrith?

Penrith has 3 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 250kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Penrith?

Charge points in Penrith are run by networks including EO Charging, POD Point (UK) and E.ON (DK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Penrith free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
