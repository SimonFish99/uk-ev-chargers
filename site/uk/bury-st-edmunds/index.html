EV charging in Bury St Edmunds — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Bury St Edmunds?

There are 15 public EV charging points in Bury St Edmunds: 0 rapid (50kW+), 7 fast (7–50kW) and 8 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Bury St Edmunds?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Bury St Edmunds right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Bury St Edmunds?

Charge points in Bury St Edmunds are run by networks including Evolt Network (Swarco E.Connect), ElectRoad (UK) and BP Pulse (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Bury St Edmunds free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
