EV charging in Scarborough — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Scarborough?

There are 12 public EV charging points in Scarborough: 1 rapid (50kW+), 11 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Scarborough?

Scarborough has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 120kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Scarborough?

Charge points in Scarborough are run by networks including Connected Kerb, POD Point (UK) and InstaVolt Ltd. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Scarborough free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
