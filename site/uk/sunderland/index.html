EV charging in Sunderland — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Sunderland?

There are 44 public EV charging points in Sunderland: 7 rapid (50kW+), 35 fast (7–50kW) and 2 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Sunderland?

Sunderland has 7 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 60kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Sunderland?

Charge points in Sunderland are run by networks including Charge Your Car, POD Point (UK) and BP Pulse (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Sunderland free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
