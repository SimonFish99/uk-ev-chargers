EV charging in Ellesmere Port — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Ellesmere Port?

There are 7 public EV charging points in Ellesmere Port: 2 rapid (50kW+), 4 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Ellesmere Port?

Ellesmere Port has 2 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 50kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Ellesmere Port?

Charge points in Ellesmere Port are run by networks including The GeniePoint Network ( EQUANS EV Solutions ), POD Point (UK) and Roam Charging (UK). You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Ellesmere Port free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
