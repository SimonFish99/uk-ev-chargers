EV charging in Tonbridge — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Tonbridge?

There are 8 public EV charging points in Tonbridge: 2 rapid (50kW+), 6 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Tonbridge?

Tonbridge has 2 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 120kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Tonbridge?

Charge points in Tonbridge are run by networks including Connected Kerb, Roam Charging (UK) and Osprey Charging. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Tonbridge free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
