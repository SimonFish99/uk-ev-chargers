How many EV charging points are there in Kent Parish?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Kent Parish right now — the fastest available is 7kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Kent Parish?

Are the EV chargers in Kent Parish free to use?
