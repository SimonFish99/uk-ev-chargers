7 stations 1 rapid (50kW+) 2 fast (7–50kW) 4 slow

EV charging in Marlow — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Marlow?

There are 7 public EV charging points in Marlow: 1 rapid (50kW+), 2 fast (7–50kW) and 4 standard (under 7kW). Between them they offer 4 different connector types.

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Marlow?

Marlow has 1 rapid charging point rated at 50kW or above. The fastest is Court Garden Car Park at 50kW. Rapid sites are labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above.

Which charging networks operate in Marlow?

Charge points in Marlow are run by 4 networks, the largest being Char.gy, Go Zero (GB) and MER. The operator is shown against each charge point in the list above.

Do I need an app to charge in Marlow?

Of the 7 charge points listed in Marlow, 1 accept payment at the charge point itself (contactless card or the operator's app) and 1 require an account, app or membership card before you can start a session. It is worth setting those accounts up before you need them rather than in the car park. Under the UK charge point regulations, rapid units of 50kW and above must display their price in pence per kWh before you start.

Are the EV chargers in Marlow free to use?

Most of the 7 charge points in Marlow require payment, and the access column above shows how each one is paid for. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for the current tariff.
