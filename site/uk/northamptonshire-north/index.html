EV charging in Northamptonshire North — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Northamptonshire North?

There are 20 public EV charging points in Northamptonshire North: 0 rapid (50kW+), 20 fast (7–50kW) and 0 standard (under 7kW).

Are there rapid chargers in Northamptonshire North?

There are no rapid (50kW+) chargers listed in Northamptonshire North right now — the fastest available is 22kW. For rapid charging, try one of the nearby towns listed further down this page.

Which charging networks operate in Northamptonshire North?

Charge points in Northamptonshire North are run by networks including Believ. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Northamptonshire North free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
