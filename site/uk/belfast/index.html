EV charging in Belfast — FAQs

How many EV charging points are there in Belfast?

There are 55 public EV charging points in Belfast: 23 rapid (50kW+), 31 fast (7–50kW) and 1 standard (under 7kW).

Where can I find rapid EV chargers in Belfast?

Belfast has 23 rapid charging points rated at 50kW or above (the fastest is 350kW). They're labelled “Rapid”, listed first on this page and pinned on the map above so you can head straight to the quickest option.

Which charging networks operate in Belfast?

Charge points in Belfast are run by networks including Weev, EZO (IE) and ChargePoint. You'll need the relevant operator's app or a contactless card to start a session at most sites.

Are the EV chargers in Belfast free to use?

Most public charge points require payment — usually via the operator's app, a contactless card or an RFID tag. Pricing varies by network and charging speed, so check the operator's app or the signage at the location for current tariffs.
